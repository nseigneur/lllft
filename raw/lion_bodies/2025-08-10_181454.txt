World Lion Day feels like the perfect excuse to talk about what’s really happening with the lions of Londolozi right now, because the dynamics are shifting, and the plotlines could not be more fascinating. Everyone in the lion world has their favourites, a bit like supporting a sports team. And just because I might think one coalition has the edge, doesn’t mean I’m wrong, it just means I’m watching the game closely. The Ndzhenga Males have held their ground on Londolozi’s eastern stretches since 2021. But time is catching up with them. They can no doubt feel the presence of younger, stronger coalitions circling the edges. They’ve clashed with the Gijima Males before, but this time, I believe it would be different. Meanwhile, the Plains Camp Males are making their presence felt in the west, and right in front of the Londolozi camps. They control a vast territory, and they’re not shy to expand. Is it land they’re after? Or the lionesses? It’s almost like a three-way chess match, except lions don’t strategise. They move on instinct, in the moment.
