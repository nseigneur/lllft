TITLE: Ndzhenga Male Fights Off Ntsevu Lioness For Wildebeest Carcass- Virtual Safari #164
DATE: 2023-04-30
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2023/04/30/ndzhenga-male-fights-off-ntsevu-lioness-for-wildebeest-carcass-virtual-safari-164/

Hearing over the radio from Alfie that he had found a Ndzhenga Male and two Ntsevu lionesses feeding on a wildebeest carcass. We get ourselves across there in anticipation of a bit of excitement as the lions feed on the carcass with the potential of internal conflict as they all try to get their fair share. It is no surprise that the male dominates the carcass and keeps the females at bay with a bout of aggression in between his feeding. Allowing us to zoom in and admire the details and intricacies of a large impressive male lion. He then lashes out at a female that is trying to nibble on the edge of the carcass before picking the entire carcass up and walking off with it.
