There’s something about the Plains Camp Males—an aura, an edginess—that keeps me on the edge of my seat whenever they’re around. They’ve been showing up at Londolozi more frequently, and on a recent evening, we thoroughly enjoyed every moment with them. After spending the afternoon with them, they finally got up and started moving. I looped ahead to position the vehicle for a classic “walk-by”—when a lion strides right past you in an open vehicle. It’s exhilarating, and humbling, and, in those fleeting moments, you feel completely at their mercy. But when an enormous male locks eyes with you from just a few feet away, the thrill turns electric. And just when we thought the moment couldn’t get more powerful, the two males belted out a full-throated roar, shaking the air around us. Over the last week, the bush seemed alive with elephants, bathed in golden afternoon light making for a few stunning sightings. Finally, to wrap it all up, we found the Ximungwe Female leopard lounging in the boughs of a jackalberry tree, her young cub at her side, the real icing on the cake.
