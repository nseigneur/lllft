While out on a relaxed bumble with some of the team, we found ourselves in the middle of a thrilling sighting involving the Kambula Pride. In the wide open grasslands, with their bellies looking rather empty, the lions were intently watching a group of kudu in the distance. Just as it seemed like a hunt was on the horizon, an unexpected visitor—a male ostrich—strolled right through the scene, temporarily distracting the lions as he wandered between them and the kudu. Luckily for the ostrich, he was too far away to become a target. After a few minutes, the kudus bolted, spooked by something unseen. But rather than chasing after them, the eldest lioness remained fixed on the exact spot from where the kudus had fled. Moments later, the entire pride sprang into action, charging across the grasslands towards the same point. When we caught up, we found an intense scene unfolding. The Kambula Pride had stolen an impala carcass from another predator, and the air was thick with tension as the lions fought amongst themselves, each trying to claim their portion of the prize.
