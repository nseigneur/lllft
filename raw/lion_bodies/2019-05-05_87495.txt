TITLE: Tsalala Cubs Alive and Well
DATE: 2019-05-05
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2019/05/05/tsalala-cubs-alive-and-well-2/

We’re just going to leave these here… Three cubs. Two females and a male apparently. Alive and well and hopefully the future of the Tsalala name. The Tsalala pride have denned cubs on these same koppies a number of times before. This litter was born on the next koppie to the south (the same one on which the Tsalala female was born) but was moved to the one they are currently on a few weeks ago. We broke the news in early March that the Tsalala female had given birth , but knowing how fragile young cubs are, no one really wanted to believe that withing a few short weeks we would be seeing tiny cubs waddling around up on Ximpalapala koppie. Yet that is exactly what has happened, and the adult lioness has chosen about as secure a den site as she could have for the latest lions of Londolozi. The big boulder strewn outcrop where the cubs are being stashed has multiple hiding places for them to scuttle into shoudl danger threaten, but the lingering smell of lioness is likely to keep many other predators away. Sightings of the cubs have been on and off, as the lioness spends much of her time away from the den hunting, but on occasion she has brought them out onto the most open rocky slope where uninterrupted views can be had. A last look-see before settling down to nurse. As exciting as this whole situation is, we’re still really only in the opening chapters; the cubs have a long, long journey ahead of them before they get even a sniff of independence, and being raised by a solitary lioness means they will have an even tougher time than most. But it’s so far, so good, and knowing what their mother has been through already just to survive and reproduce, we wouldn’t count against them…
