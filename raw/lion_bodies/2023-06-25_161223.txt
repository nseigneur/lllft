DATE: 2023-06-25

Join us on an exhilarating afternoon game drive as we follow the Ntsevu Breakaway Pride as they trail the large herd of buffalo hoping to snag themselves a hefty meal. The Sun sets and as darkness sets in, the conditions begin to favour the lions more so than the buffalo. We strap ourselves in for the long hall knowing very well that the lions will at least have a go after a buffalo. Without wanting to affect the hunt in any way we choose to sit at a distance and switch off all our lights and solely rely on our hearing to be able to tell when something is going down. After a long wait, we hear the herd erupt and know that it’s go time for the lions. From here we then watch an intense battle between the lions and the buffalo as they try to rescue the young one that has been caught and spend the next while watching lions and buffalo charging all over the show until the lions eventually walk out victorious.
