There’s nothing quite as invigorating as waking up to the bellowing roars of two male lions in close proximity to the camp. Promptly gathering ourselves, we ventured into the bush to see what was unfolding. It wasn’t long before Robyn beat us to it and found two male lions sitting proudly on the edge of a large clearing. With it only having just gone 05h00, still trying to wipe the sleep out of our eyes and the lions resting with lowered heads or facing away from us, discerning their identity proved challenging. Initially, we speculated that they might be the Ndzhenga Males, given our location. However, as they raised their heads, it became apparent that these were, in fact, the Plains Camp Males. Their sudden arrival in the area had been prompted by some event, leading them to assert their dominance and drive away any other lions in the vicinity. A few days earlier, the Ntsevu Breakaway Pride had been in the same area, leaving us to ponder whether these males were chasing them, the Skorro Breakaway Male, or perhaps the Ndzhenga Males. As they settled down, a sizable elephant bull entered the scene, prompting the lions to rise and move a few hundred meters down the trail before settling again briefly. Before we knew it, they set off on a territorial patrol, traversing Ximpalapala Crest—a region renowned for offering the property’s most breathtaking views. Don’t waste any time, watch the video and join us as we follow these imposing male lions in broad daylight through open clearings, surrounded by incredible vistas and a prominent koppie as a backdrop; the experience couldn’t have been more remarkable.
