# Lions of Londolozi – reference

Generated from `data/lions.json`. Reconstructed from narrative posts in https://blog.londolozi.com/category/lions-2/ – there is no official lion database. Lions are mostly unnamed; labels are the blog's own. Entries marked `inferred` are not stated outright in a post.

Counts: 19 prides, 36 coalitions, 135 individuals/cohorts, 366 relationships, 66 open questions.

> Note: a few prides/coalitions appear under slightly different names because three extraction passes overlapped (e.g. "Msuthlu Pride" and "Msuthlu Pride (formerly Talamati Breakaway Pride) - link only"). They are kept unmerged rather than guessed together.

### Tsalala Pride
* Aliases: Two and Seven Pride (1998); Two and Four Pride; One and Two; Tsalala core pride (as opposed to the Tsalala Breakaway prides); Tsalala "Pride" (the lone Tsalala Female, 2021-)
* Status: Effectively a single lioness (Tsalala Female, b. 2019), alive at last mention (Feb 2026); no surviving cubs since 2019. About 11 lions in 2009 and 10 in 2011; down to one female by June 2018.
* First/last mentioned: 2015-09-28 / 2026-02-08
* Origin: Formed in 1998 by two Castleton Pride females (believed pregnant by the West Street Males) who broke away and denned seven cubs near Taylor's Crossing. Named for Tsalala (Gardenia) Pan. Only one cub (the Original Tailless Female) outlived the founders; her descendants are the Tsalala lineage (Legacy post).
  * 1998: Two females break away from the Castleton Pride and den seven cubs ("Two and Seven").
  * 1999-2002: Attrition to "One and Two": cubs killed by Sparta Pride, hyenas, injury, poaching; one founder kicked by a zebra; remaining founder killed by hyenas Dec 2002 leaving the Original Tailless Female alone.
  * 2002-12: Original Tailless Female gives birth to twin daughters (the Tsalala Sisters) at Ximpalapala Koppie.
  * 2005: Original Tailless Female loses her tail to hyenas at a zebra kill.
  * 2007: Mapogo Males (six) take over, kill the Rocky Male and all but two of the Sisters' 2006 cubs.
  * 2008-2009: Pride thrives under two Mapogo males (Kinky-tail, Satan); litters in 2008 and 2009 bring it to 11.
  * 2010-06: Majingilane Males arrive, kill several cubs; the two Sisters join them; the Original Tailless Female leaves with four 2009-born sub-adult females (first Tsalala Breakaway Pride).
  * 2011-10: Breakaway rejoins briefly; reunion with the Sisters fails; Original Tailless Female returns to natal pride in Nov 2011.
  * 2011-11-01: Younger Sister loses her tail to hyenas; eight 2011 cubs reduced to one by May 2012.
  * 2012: Four sub-adult females form the independent Mhangeni Pride.
  * 2013: Original Tailless Female killed by the Mhangeni lionesses; Tailed Sister bears the 2013 litter (3 M, 1 F).
  * 2015-09: Matimba Males arrive; pride splits; Tailless Sister takes four sub-adults away (second Tsalala Breakaway Pride).
  * 2016-03/06: Matimba-sired litters: 2 cubs (Tailed Sister) and 3 cubs (2011 Female).
  * 2017-08: Core pride and breakaway pride feed together (11 lions) - temporary reunion.
  * 2017-10: 2011 Female and cubs lost; Tailed Sister joins the Tailless Sister in the breakaway.
  * 2018-02: Tailed Sister found dead.
  * 2018-06: Tailless Sister dies at Marthly Pools; the 2013-born female is the sole Tsalala lioness.
  * 2019-03: First litter of the 2013-born female (3 cubs; father a Birmingham male); cubs reduced to one by Aug 2019.
  * 2021-11-20: The 2013-born female is killed in the western Sabi Sand; her daughter (b. 2019) is left alone.
  * 2022-08/09: Daughter pairs with a Mhangeni lioness for ~2 months.
  * 2024-05/11: Daughter's first litter (3 cubs, Plains Camp-sired) is lost.
  * 2025-09: Second litter lost within a week.
* Notes: The matriline: Original Tailless Female (b.1998) -> Tsalala Sisters (b.2002; Tailless & Tailed) -> Tsalala 2011 Female / Tsalala 2013 Female (+3 brothers) -> Tsalala Female b.2019 (cubs 2024, 2025). The blog repeatedly stresses that single mothers raising a single cub recurs across generations (Original Tailless 2002; 2013 female 2019). first_mentioned/last_mentioned = first and last post in the 307-file corpus matching the group name (not the group's active period).

### Ntsevu Pride
* Aliases: Mhangeni Breakaway Pride / Mhangeni Breakaways (until 29 Apr 2017); Mhangeni sub-adult females; Kambula Pride (the name Mala Mala used for the same pride, per 2024-03-21); Nstevu / Ntesvu (blog misspellings)
* Status: active (post-2024 'Ntsevu' = smaller faction of 3 original lionesses plus Ndzhenga-sired cubs, mostly north of / east of the Sand River; still mating with Gijima Males in 2025-26)
* First/last mentioned: 2016-08-20 / 2026-08-23
* Origin: Six lionesses (plus three brothers) of the first Mhangeni litter (born 2013, sired by the Majingilane) who split from their mothers (the Mhangeni Pride) as sub-adults ~2015-16 and were renamed 'Ntsevu' ('six' in Shangaan) on 2017-04-29. Mhangeni -> Tsalala -> Castleton lineage. Mated with Matshipiri, Matimba, Majingilane then settled with the Birmingham Males (2018-2021), then Ndzhenga Males (Nov 2021-Aug 2025), then Gijima Males (from Aug 2025).
  * 2017-04-29: Mhangeni Breakaway pride formally renamed the Ntsevu Pride; six lionesses no longer rejoining the adult Mhangeni females.
  * 2017-07-03: Ntsevu pushed the Sparta pride out of the central/south-east range and killed one Sparta lioness.
  * 2018-04-28: Birmingham Males (4) become the Ntsevu's dominant/mating coalition (mating observed for months; first cubs 2018).
  * 2021-11-25: Ndzhenga Males (4) arrive in early Nov 2021; the surviving Birmingham Male is pushed out; Ntsevu mothers flee east/north with their 11 cubs, 7 lost.
  * 2021-08-04: 11-12 sub-adults (6 males, 5-6 females; born mid-2018) leave the pride to become the Ntsevu Breakaway Pride.
  * 2022-04-11: Two Ntsevu mothers (the 'Ntsevu Two') reappear with the last 4 Birmingham-sired cubs, having broken away from the main pride after the Ndzhenga arrival.
  * 2024-03-21: Pride officially divided in two: smaller faction (3 original lionesses, living in northern Mala Mala) keeps the name Ntsevu; larger faction (1 original + 4/5 Birmingham-sired daughters + Ndzhenga-sired cubs) named Kambula Pride.
  * 2025-11-20: After the Gijima Males push north (Aug-Nov 2025), the 3-lioness Ntsevu Pride with 3 cubs follows the Ndzhenga Males north; one Gijima Male mates with a Ntsevu lioness (Aug, Sep, Nov 2025).
* Notes: Pre-2024 blog posts use 'Ntsevu' for the whole pride (6 founding lionesses + cubs). From 24 Mar 2024 the name is restricted to the smaller faction. Counts of cubs/sub-adults conflict between posts (see open_questions).

### Sparta Pride

### Talamati Pride
* Aliases: Nkuhuma Breakaway Pride (2007-2011 name); Talamati lionesses
* Status: Active but without fixed territory in 2023; 2023: five lionesses (oldest 'Silver-eye', ~15 yrs in 2022, plus four ~5-year-olds) and two young males who had separated from the pride. Related 'Talamati Breakaway Pride' = Msuthlu Pride (2025).
* First/last mentioned: 2016-04-02 / 2025-10-05
* Origin: Broke away from the Nkuhuma Pride in 2007 when the Mapogo took over from the Manyeleti Males; initially the Nkuhuma Breakaway Pride, taken over by the 'Old Nkuhuma Males' who sired two females (incl. the one-eyed 'Silver-eye'); dispersed to Kruger NP in 2008 with those males; named after Talamati Bushveld Camp in 2011; returned to the Sabi Sands in 2011.
  * 2022-07-25: Succession of coalitions: Matimba Males (2011-2015) -> Selati Males (2015) -> Northern Avoca Males (2019); Matimba and Selati daughters later mate with the Imbali Male (2021).
  * 2022-07-25: First arrival on Londolozi mid-April 2022: 8 lions (2 adult females + 2 young males + 4 young females; ninth is Dark-maned Northern Avoca male).
  * 2023-08-07: Aug 2023: pride has no territory of its own; four young lionesses + oldest lioness; two young males separate.
  * 2025-10-05: Oct 2025: 'Msuthlu Pride (formerly the Talamati Breakaway Pride)' is mentioned as returning to Londolozi with the Gijima Males.
* Notes: first_mentioned 2016-04-02 refers to nomadic Talamati MALES only; pride first seen on Londolozi mid-Apr 2022. ### Msuthlu Pride
* Aliases: Talamati Breakaway Pride (former name, per 2025-10-05)
* Status: active; one of the most-viewed prides on Londolozi 2025-26, now fragmenting (Sep 2026)
* First/last mentioned: 2025-09-22 / 2026-09-01
* Origin: Described as 'formerly the Talamati Breakaway Pride' (2025-10-05). First met in 2022 in the north of the reserve (per 2026-04-04) and later settled in the far south of the Sabi Sand under the Gijima Males. Originally 4 adult lionesses and their offspring, 14 lions in total, younger members sired by the Gijima Males.
  * 2025-09-22: Gijima Males named as dominant over the Styx, Southern and Msuthlu prides; pride starts following them north.
  * 2025-10-08: Furthest north yet; kill a buffalo with the Gijima Males; Gijima Males named as fathers of the pride's cubs.
  * 2026-04-04: With the Ndzhenga and Gijima males swapping territory, Msuthlu become the 'resident pride roaming central Londolozi': 3 adult lionesses, 2 sub-adult males, 2 sub-adult females and 3 younger cubs seen most often.
  * 2026-08-13: Adult females come into heat and mate with the N'waswitsaka (Nwaswitshaka) Males in the south; 11 known members.
  * 2026-09-01: Pride no longer cohesive; Gijima Males moved north, Nwaswitshaka Males prominent in south; they harass a young Msuthlu male while two adult females defend him.
* Notes: Exact link to the Talamati Pride (broke from Nkuhuma 2007) is only stated as 'formerly the Talamati Breakaway Pride' in one post; see open_questions.

### Styx Pride
* Aliases: Styx lionesses
* Status: active in the south (Gijima/Ndzhenga territory)
* First/last mentioned: 2015-12-14 / 2025-09-22
* Origin: Old pride of 14 lions at Londolozi in 2004 (Shaws Males took over Styx and Sparta prides in 2006). 2018-2020: visited Londolozi's north from the north-east, semi-nomadic by Aug 2020 after losing male cover. Later (2022) described as '3 adults and 11 cubs' established south of Londolozi, covered by the Ndzhenga Males who sired litters with them; by 2025 covered by the Gijima Males.
  * 2020-08-18: Styx pride semi-vagrant, without a dominant male; joined by one Nkuhuma young male.
  * 2022-08-10: Styx Pride: 3 adults, 11 cubs; moved south into old Ndzhenga territory, covered by Ndzhenga Males.
  * 2022-11-10: Ndzhenga Males had sired several litters with the Styx Pride before moving north.
  * 2025-09-22: Now dominated by the Gijima Males (along with Southern and Msuthlu prides).
* Notes: 'Styx Male' (b. 2021) is a son of the Ndzhenga Males and a Styx-pride litter. Separate from the older 'Styx Males' coalition of 2014-15 and 'Styx Young Male (b.2016)' who broke away with an Nkuhuma young male.
