What a week it has been at Londolozi, filled with unforgettable moments and thrilling sightings. Choosing which highlights to include in this Virtual Safari was no easy task, but here’s a taste of the action! First, we witnessed the return of the legendary Tsalala Female lioness. After a lengthy absence and the heartbreak of losing her cubs, she’s likely to be moving more frequently, and we were lucky to catch a glimpse of her resilience in action. The Makomsava Female leopard, spotted in the north, teased us with the possibility of leading us back to her den, where her week-old cubs are hidden. Meanwhile, the Ximungwe Female leopard, who is also nursing a new litter, has moved her cubs to an unknown location. After her successful duiker kill, we waited, hoping she might lead us to them. To top it all off, an evening game drive brought us face-to-face with the tension and drama of lion dynamics—a heated standoff between the Ndzhenga Males and the Plains Camp Males kept us on the edge of our seats.
