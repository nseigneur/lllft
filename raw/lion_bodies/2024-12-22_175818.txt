TITLE: Lions’ Surprise Visitor and Stunning Leopard Cubs- Virtual Safari #250
DATE: 2024-12-22
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2024/12/22/virtual-safari-250/

This week’s Virtual Safari takes us on an incredible journey with the resilient Tsalala Female Lion and her growing connection with the dominant Plains Camp Males. Since returning to Londolozi, the Tsalala Female has become a regular feature, and we’ve been fortunate to observe her in breathtaking settings atop Ximpalapala Crest. Having recently lost her cubs, she’s likely to come into heat soon, explaining why the Plains Camp Males are sticking close by. But the highlight of this sighting? A surprising little visitor managed to sneak right past the nose of the Plains Camp Male without being noticed! The magic doesn’t stop there. While exploring the northern parts of Londolozi, we spend time with a majestic elephant bull and a flock of Southern Ground Hornbills. And finally, the morning ends with a heartwarming sighting of the Ximungwe Female Leopard and her cub. Could you tell if the cub is a male or a female?
