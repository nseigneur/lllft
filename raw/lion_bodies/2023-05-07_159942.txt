TITLE: Male Lions Wade Into Crocodile Infested Water While Elephants Watch- Virtual Safari #165
DATE: 2023-05-07
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2023/05/07/male-lions-walk-into-crocodile-infested-water-while-elephants-watch-virtual-safari-165/

There are two new male lions starting to make a name for themselves on Londolozi, the Black Dam Males. After finding them calling and scent-marking in the morning near the Londolozi airstrip, deep in the Ndzhenga Males’ territory, they appeared to be on the trail of one of the Talamati Lionesses. We head out in the afternoon with the hopes that they will continue to follow the trail of the lioness which will lead them towards the Sand River, around Finfoot Crossing. To see male lions cross the river is always phenomenal, but shortly after arriving with them the two males stand up and start walking towards the river in response to hearing a lioness call, leading us to get so much more than we bargained for.
