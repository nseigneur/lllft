This week had more lion drama than I was expecting. It started with the Kambula Pride on a wildebeest carcass not far from camp. The hyenas arrived first, calling for backup, trying to work up the numbers to challenge the lions. That didn’t work. But what it did bring in was the three Ntsevu Lionesses. There is a lot of history between these two prides, and they certainly left nothing on the table. Before I get into what happened when those two prides met, it’s worth noting that the Ntsevu Pride had already tried their luck at a different carcass earlier in the week, and that one didn’t go their way either. There are also a couple of other videos from the team this week, including a Gijima Male in a position we would not have expected to see him in, and a very welcome update on the Nkoveni cubs.
