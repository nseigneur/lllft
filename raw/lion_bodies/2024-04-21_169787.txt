The Ntsevu Breakaway Lions have been garnering attention over the past few months. The males are growing into impressive-looking lions, with their size and stature skyrocketing recently. Some of the males have encountered females on neighbouring reserves, prompting them to mate with a few, which, in my opinion, has caused the males to increase drastically in size. Being a coalition of four bodes well for their future territorial establishment. However, the unfortunate reality is that, although they are blessed with fantastic genes, they will likely be some of the largest lions around here when fully mature, but they lack the experience to overthrow any of the current dominant coalitions. Recently, they found themselves in a few altercations that thankfully did not result in any severe injuries but will serve as great learning curves and be invaluable in the long run. They need to remain under the radar for a little while longer to allow them to grow and develop further. If all four can mature, they will be a force to be reckoned with.
