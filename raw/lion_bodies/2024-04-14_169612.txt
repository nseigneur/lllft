At the end of last week, I spent some time with the Kambula Pride after they had just finished a zebra meal during a spell of light rain. The overcast, cool weather suggested they might be on the move. My luck held out when two large elephant bulls appeared nearby, prompting the lions to get up and start moving. The next morning, the Kambula Pride was found again, about 2km from where we last saw them. This time, the pride’s 13 cubs were full of energy and ready to play. They were chasing, tackling each other, and eventually climbing a Cassia tree—a tree I’d previously seen the Nkoveni Female leopard and her two cubs in. This offered a great chance to compare how leopards and lions navigate tree climbing and showcased the playful, lively side of the Kambula cubs.
