TITLE: Introducing the New Beastly Male Lions To Londolozi- Virtual Safari #177
DATE: 2023-07-30
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2023/07/30/161980/

In today’s Virtual Safari, we are thrilled to present the majestic Gijima Male Lions for the very first time as they feast upon a zebra carcass. These impressive lions have recently made their presence known in the area to the south of Londolozi and are believed to be responsible for the injuries that led to the demise of one of the Ndzhenga Males after an altercation many months ago. Although they have frequented the southernmost reaches of Londolozi, they have not been seen this far north into Londolozi and are yet to feature on our Virtual Safari series. The Gijima Males, known for their imposing stature and commanding presence, are potentially here to stay. Their arrival has stirred excitement and curiosity among the rangers and trackers, what is next in the saga of the Lions of Londolozi? With the Gijima Males potentially here to stay, this sighting becomes even more significant, offering us a glimpse into their lives and the possible impact on the local lion dynamics. We’ll be following their journey closely and hope to have a few more sightings of these incredible males in the near future. Stay tuned!
