TITLE: More Tree Climbing Lions- Virtual Safari #159
DATE: 2023-03-26
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2023/03/26/more-tree-climbing-lions-virtual-safari-159/

While out on a morning game drive we received a radio call from Kelsey informing us that she had found a number of lions in the southwestern grasslands after a successful tracking mission. As they were following the lions, one female ran ahead and climbed into a marula tree. Now we know there are only a few reasons lions would choose to climb into a tree and going after a carcass was the case in this particular sighting. Not just any carcass but an ostrich of all animals. Being a little distance away, I did my best to get across to where they were in the hopes of seeing the lioness still in the tree.
