Driving out of camp first thing in the morning is always filled with excitement. You are setting out on an endeavour to discover what activity has unfolded during the course of the evening, armed with a hot water bottle, a fluffy fleece blanket and adrenaline to keep you warm on a chilly winter’s morning. Before you know it you are in the company of the full complement of the Kambula Pride and the two Ndzhenga Males feasting on a wildebeest on the Londolozi airstrip. There are few better ways to start off your day out in the African Bushveld. As they were finishing up the last remains of the feast, we watched a few youngsters playing around and having a whale of time, before the entire contingent set off towards a nearby waterhole for a drink. Presenting us with a fantastic view of so many lions surrounding a waterhole quenching their thirst.
