Experience the delicate balance of life in the African bush as we witness both the miracle of birth and the reality of survival. We begin by reflecting on last week’s extraordinary moment—witnessing an impala ewe bring new life into the world. The sight of her tiny lamb taking its first tentative steps before confidently trotting alongside its mother was truly remarkable. But the bush tells no fairy tales. We then joined the Three Rivers Duo where the Young Female demonstrates the counterpoint to new life, dragging one of three impala lambs that she and her mother successfully hunted in a single evening. This predation, though seemingly harsh, plays perfectly into the impala’s survival strategy. Their synchronized birthing season creates such an abundance of lambs that while many fall prey to waiting predators, the majority survive to sustain their thriving populations. And the cycle continues. Two Kambula Lionesses now carry the promise of new life, with one visibly heavy with cubs. The anticipation builds as we await the next generation of Londolozi’s royal family, knowing that soon we might be privileged to document tiny lion cubs taking their first steps into this wild world.
