This week’s virtual safari starts off with a little update on the birding big year front as we flash through a number of the birds I have seen, I must add I have not taken the competition as seriously as others, I am only on about 110 birds, but that I have filmed while on Londolozi as opposed to having just seen them. We then get ourselves down to Finfoot Crossing ahead of a male lion, initially thought to be one of the Ntsevu Males but later discovered to be the Skorro Breakaway Male. While set up ready and waiting for him to cross through, he was a no-show. Only a herd of elephants came to the party, forcing me to question if the other rangers knew the difference between a lion and an elephant. Thankfully they did as the lion turned up much further upstream. To finish off the Virtual Safari we put together a summary-based collection of a few of the other sightings that weren’t strong enough to do as a stand-alone Virtual Safari.
