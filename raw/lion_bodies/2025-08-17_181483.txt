TITLE: Chaos on the Airstrip: Gijima Males Rise as Ndzhengas Fade | Virtual Safari #284
DATE: 2025-08-17

It is all happening at Londolozi right now. In fact, our predictions from World Lion Day are already starting to take shape and sooner than we thought. The Gijima Males have made their presence felt in a big way, storming onto the Londolozi Airstrip to chase off the Nomads, the Nkuhuma Male and Talamati Male, before taking over and mating with one of the Ntsevu Lionesses . These were wild, chaotic scenes that perfectly capture how quickly power can shift out here. And all the while, the Ndzhenga Males —once the dominant force in the east—are nowhere to be seen. The story began with the Styx Male and a Kambula Breakaway lioness near camp. That night, the Nomads linked up with a Ntsevu Lioness and began mating boldly on the airstrip, roaring into the darkness as though already laying claim to new territory. But those roars carried. The Gijima Males, hearing the challenge, had already begun marching north. By Friday evening, we left the Talamati Male still mating on the airstrip, knowing that if he dared to roar, the Gijimas would almost certainly come charging in. And sure enough, they did. But the story didn’t end there. Under the cover of darkness, when no one was watching, more clashes unfolded. By morning, one Gijima Male bore a gash on his face and one of the Plains Camp Males was limping, his back leg injured. Proof that the battles between coalitions are no longer looming—they are already happening. The lion dynamics at Londolozi are shifting before our eyes. Rivalries are igniting. Old kings are fading.
