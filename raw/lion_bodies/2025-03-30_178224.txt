This week’s Virtual Safari brings us a morning of surprises, instinct, and new life. We start by spending time with the Xinkhova Female, who moves silently through the long grass, her sharp eyes scanning for any opportunity to hunt. With her sleek movements and natural camouflage, she disappears into the golden landscape, edging closer to something scuttling through the undergrowth. As we watch, we’re reminded just how unpredictable a leopard’s hunt can be. From there, we head to the Kambula Lioness’s den, where her tiny cubs are out in the open, giving us a heartwarming glimpse of their playful nature. But what we discover next is unusual—the mother has made a kill inside the den. This is a risky decision, as the scent of a carcass could attract unwanted predators and put the cubs in danger. Why would she take such a chance? I guess when an easy hunting opportunity presents itself, it is hard to say no. To end the morning, we witness something truly special—a newborn elephant calf, only hours old, stumbling through its first few steps. As we arrive, the mother elephant is still delivering the afterbirth, marking the incredible moment where new life begins.
