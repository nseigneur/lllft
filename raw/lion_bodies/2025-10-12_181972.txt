TITLE: A Perfect Safari Morning: Lions, Elephants, Rhinos & A Leopard| Virtual Safari #292
DATE: 2025-10-12
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2025/10/12/a-perfect-safari-morning-lions-elephants-rhinos-a-leopard-virtual-safari-292/

This week’s Virtual Safari focuses on a single, pretty epic morning, as opposed to the usual entire week. We start with one of the Gijima Males , lying nearby with a belly so full he could hardly move. Both males had been feeding on a buffalo carcass alongside two Kambula Lionesses , but as the morning warmed up, the blonde Gijima Male wandered off to find a quieter patch of shade. Then, in true bush magic fashion, a herd of elephants moved through the background, providing a breathtaking scene, a powerful male lion in the foreground, framed by elephants in the distance. And just when we thought it couldn’t get any better, the lion got up and strolled straight past a group of three rhinos . Three of the Big Five, all within 50 metres, moments like these are what make living and working out here so extraordinary. Just as we were about to wrap up, a call came through the radio from Shaun D’Araujo — impala alarm calling not far away. We rushed over to find the Three Rivers Female Leopard on the move, marching through a clearing and clearly in hunting mode. Following her through the bush rounded off the drive perfectly.
