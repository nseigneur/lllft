TITLE: Lions On The Move- Virtual Safari #236
DATE: 2024-09-15
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2024/09/15/lions-on-the-move-virtual-safari-236/

This morning’s game drive began with a thrilling sight of the two Ndzhenga Males, moving purposefully into the wind, clearly tracking something carried on the breeze. Word from our neighbours suggested that another group of lions had been following a herd of buffalo, which might have been what drew these males in that direction. Their focused determination allowed us to predict their path, getting us ahead of them several times. This gave us the incredible experience of watching these two powerful males stride right past our vehicle several times before heading down into the Sand River. Afterwards, we returned to where the Ndzhenga Males had first begun their mission, only to be greeted by a spectacular photographic moment. The Kambula Pride, in the middle of the Monzo Sheet Erosion, was moving through the area’s vast open clearing, the short grass offering a perfect view.
