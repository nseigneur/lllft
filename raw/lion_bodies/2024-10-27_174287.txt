TITLE: Three New Coalitions, One Territory: What’s Next for Londolozi’s Lions? Virtual Safari #242
DATE: 2024-10-27
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2024/10/27/three-new-coalitions-one-territory-whats-next-for-londolozis-lions-virtual-safari-242/

Lion dynamics at Londolozi are as thrilling as they are unpredictable. The constant ebb and flow of territories, shifting coalitions, and the ever-changing dominance of male lions create an intricate web that is always in motion. It’s a challenge to follow, yet that’s precisely what makes it so captivating. On any given game drive, we never really know what we’ll encounter, and it’s this uncertainty that keeps us on our toes. In this Virtual Safari, we witness the unfolding drama of three distinct coalitions, each with its own story to tell. We start with a bold move by two Nkuhlu Males, venturing deep into the territory of the dominant Ndzhenga Males to feast on a giraffe carcass. Next, we meet two old nomads—the Southern Avoca and Kruger Males—who have joined forces out of necessity. Though past their prime, their bond grants them a better chance of survival, allowing them to hunt more effectively and fend off potential threats. Finally, the return of the Ntsevu Males (also known as the Kambula Males) brings an air of regality to the southern grasslands. Having roamed far to the north, they now emerge looking more formidable than ever.
