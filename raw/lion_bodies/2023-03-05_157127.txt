TITLE: Lions in Incredible Light Show Us That There Are More Cubs- Virtual Safari #156
DATE: 2023-03-05
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2023/03/05/lions-in-incredible-light-show-us-that-there-are-more-cubs-virtual-safari-156/

During a leisurely afternoon bumble without any set plan, we happened to venture into the territory where the Nstevu Pride was reputed to be nurturing their cubs. To our delight, we chanced upon a mesmerizing sight: three Ntsevu lionesses and a Ndzhenga male basking in the midst of lush greenery, an impressive herd of impala in the background. We were content with how the afternoon was shaping out to be. Until we heard the calls of another lioness just behind the vehicle! Sitting in the vehicle at that moment was an amazing experience as the lionesses quickly got up and started moving towards the calls of the approaching lioness. Their excitement was evident as they eagerly greeted and brushed up against a fourth female (who had suckle marks!), right beside the vehicle.
