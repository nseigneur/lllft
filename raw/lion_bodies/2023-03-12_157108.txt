On our way across to a sighting of what was initially two lionesses, we get slightly distracted by an incredible scene of a huge dazzle of zebra and journey of giraffe. One too spectacular to simply drive past. So we opt to sit back and enjoy as they all move through an open clearing. It’s often in taking time to sit with such magnificent animals you get to appreciate some of the smaller more intricate details. One of which is how a giraffe drinks. Imagine if your head was six metres above the ground, just how challenging it is to quench your thirst. We get to watch this a number of times as well as a few of the zebra charging across the open ground, rolling around. A mesmerising scene in its entirety.
