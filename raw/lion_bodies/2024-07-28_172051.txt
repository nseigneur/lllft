TITLE: Plains Camp Males Push Deep Into Ndzhenga Males’ Territory- Virtual Safari #229
DATE: 2024-07-28
URL: https://blog.londolozi.com/2024/07/28/plains-camp-males-push-deep-into-ndzhenga-males-territory-virtual-safari-229/

The Plains Camp Males are beginning to apply a lot more pressure on the Ndzhenga Males by pushing further east into the territory. A recent sighting of a dead hippo at the causeway lured them into the area where they picked up on the scent of some other lions, most likely the Ntsevu Pride. This led to these enormous male lions continuing further east in pursuit of these females until they reached Plaque Rock, the furthest east they have been seen. After a short rest to regather themselves, they got up and swiftly returned west to the carcass. Spending the morning with two large male lions and having them walking alongside the vehicle is certainly a magical moment. The lion dynamics going forward are going to keep us all guessing and it is going go to be interesting to see what unfolds and how much further the Plains Camp Males push.
